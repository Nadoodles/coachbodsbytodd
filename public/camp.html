About Coach Todd's Youth Football Programs

Coaching young athletes in the Pacific Palisades since 2008.

Coach Todd's Youth Football Academy is built on one idea: football is a vehicle for building better kids, not just better players. Through structured, age-appropriate coaching, young athletes ages 6–13 develop real skills alongside confidence, discipline, and teamwork.
