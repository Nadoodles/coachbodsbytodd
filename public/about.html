Meet Coach Todd

The person behind Bods by Todd: coach, trainer, and mentor in Los Angeles.

His Story

A Coach Who Shows Up For Every Person He Trains

Coach Todd has spent 26 years coaching youth football and 28 years as a personal fitness trainer in Los Angeles. A former Division 1 college football player at Marshall University, he brings the discipline of a competitor and the patience of a mentor to every session — whether he's coaching a 6-year-old's first snap or guiding a parent through their first workout in years.

Coaching Career

Nearly Three Decades in Los Angeles Football & Fitness

  • Head Trainer, Palisades YMCA (2006–present)
  • Youth Football Director, Palisades YMCA (2006–2019)
  • Assistant Head Coach & Offensive Coordinator, Venice High School (2006–2012)
  • Head of Football Operations & 8th Grade Head Coach, Calvary Christian School (2012–present)
  • Founder, Palisades Ultimate Football League — "PUF'League" (2015–2022)
  • Youth Football Director & Head Coach, LA Rams — 14 camp venues across Southern California, ages 6–14 (2017–2018)
  • Head Football Coach, Palisades High School (2022)
  • Private coach for high school football players (ongoing)
